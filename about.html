About Attime.in

Attime.in is built with a simple mission: make attendance management effortless for Indian businesses. Started in Delhi, we understand the real challenges of SMEs, startups, schools and enterprises — proxy attendance, payroll errors, lack of real-time data.

Our platform combines biometric hardware, GPS-based mobile attendance, and a beautiful cloud dashboard to give you 100% accurate, secure, and compliant attendance data.

Why Attime?

  • Affordable - starts at ₹499/month
  • Secure - ISO 27001 ready infrastructure
  • Support - Hindi + English support via WhatsApp
